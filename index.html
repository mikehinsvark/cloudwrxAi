01What does a Dreams agent actually do?

You introduce business owners using your personal referral link. The provider handles the fit check and licensing conversation. You do not have to value a dataset, negotiate a data license, or upload company records on this page.

02How do I get my own referral link?

Use any Become an agent button to visit the Cloudwrx partner page with this Dreams referral connection. Continue through the provider’s signup and review the affiliate agreement. HistoryPays manages the link, tracking, and affiliate payments.

03Are the commission examples guaranteed?

No. The dollar amounts on this page are illustrative scenarios, not an earnings forecast or a promise of a deal. Eligibility, completed transactions, provider fees, and your written agreement determine actual commissions. The provider’s examples use different team assumptions; the calculator makes those assumptions explicit.

04When would I receive a commission?

The source program says agents are paid after the company has been paid. A referral is not a completed transaction. Payment timing, eligibility, and exact rates should be reviewed in your written agreement; there is no promised payment date here.

05Which businesses are worth a conversation?

Businesses with desk-based teams, repeat workflows, documented decisions, and several years of operational history may be worth exploring. Software, accounting, legal, operations, and industrial businesses are examples—not an approved list or a guarantee of qualification. The provider determines fit.

06What about privacy and client information?

Begin with a high-level introduction, not private records. Any data sharing needs the business owner’s approval, rights review, and appropriate privacy safeguards. CPAs and other licensed professionals should review board rules on referral fees and must not use client tax-return information to make introductions.
