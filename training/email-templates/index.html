Professional firm: full letterLaw, accounting, and consulting · address the real questions+
Make it yours before sending.
Subject: A records-licensing conversation for [FIRM NAME] Hi [FIRST NAME], Hope all is well. [PERSONAL CONTEXT] I wanted to introduce an idea for your firm to review: licensing suitable operational records for AI. Examples to discuss might include internal SOPs, IT tickets, intake processes, and administrative workflows separable from restricted client material. We work with CloudWRX to introduce businesses for a fit review. I’m a referral partner and may be compensated if a deal closes. Your firm’s rights and professional obligations come first. PRIVILEGE AND CONFIDENTIALITY Privileged client files, identities, protected health information, account details, and restricted material should stay out. Your counsel should determine whether an operational subset can be licensed. PRIVACY AND PERMITTED USE Ask how exclusions, de-identification, human review, buyer access, retention, deletion, and re-identification restrictions are handled. Removing names alone does not establish anonymity. ACCESS AND EFFORT The first discussion is about your business and systems. Please do not send me records, logins, or passwords. If you proceed, authorized teams should agree on any export and review process in writing. COST AND VALUE The brochure describes no upfront owner fee and a success-based model. Confirm all current fees and conditions in the agreement. Value and timing depend on fit, buyer review, and terms; I won’t guess what your firm would receive. Could you send two or three times for a three-way introduction? Your managing partners, ethics counsel, and IT lead are welcome. [YOUR NAME] [PHONE]
