Process & pay

Keep the owner journey, the partner journey, and the two referral links clear.

Four stages. An informed owner at every step.

  1. Describe the business

    Start with staff, systems, years of records, and the decision maker. A fit conversation does not require raw files or passwords.

  2. Review proposed terms

    Discuss scope and any non-binding valuation or letter of intent. Understand fees, buyer identity, permitted use, and obligations before committing.

  3. Prepare and review

    If the owner proceeds, the provider and authorized company team handle agreed exports, de-identification, exclusions, and review.

  4. Complete and pay

    Payment depends on buyer approval and the signed terms. Partner payment follows payment to the company.

Timing update

The current provider page says usually 90–120 days after signing. The original brochure says about 60 days from introduction. Use current guidance and written terms; never promise a payment date. Checked November 23, 2026.

Two tracks. Two different links.

Business owners

Use your client link for companies that may be a fit. Introduce the owner or arrange a three-way discovery call.

Strategic partners

Use your partner link for people who want to introduce owners. Start with the Dreams overview, then the partner program.

Get your own links

Your affiliate account supplies your personal links. Confirm that each one credits you correctly before sharing. Open affiliate login.

Understand compensation before you introduce.

RelationshipHow the provider describes it
Direct referralA share of fees on a completed deal. The provider gives a $50,000 referral example on a $500,000 deal.
Affiliate you introduceThe provider gives a 10% example on that affiliate’s commission.
Payment and ratesPaid through HistoryPays after the company is paid. Your agreement states actual rates and conditions.

Provider examples checked November 23, 2026. They do not establish typical earnings. Review current partner information.

Ground rules for every conversation

  • Lead with the owner’s interest; educate without pressure.
  • Say “AI labs.” Do not suggest a named lab is a buyer for this company.
  • Disclose referral compensation.
  • Do not promise an amount, approval, or payment date.
  • Do not collect business records, logins, or passwords.
  • If you do not know, bring the question to the provider team.
