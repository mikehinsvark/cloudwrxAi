The opportunity

Understand what makes everyday business knowledge valuable—and explain it without the hype.

THE IDEA IN TWO SENTENCES

Established businesses have years of records that show how real work happens. CloudWRX helps explore whether a buyer may license suitable operational records for AI, subject to fit, rights, privacy review, and the owner’s written agreement.

Work leaves a trail of know-how.

Decisions in context

Email threads and team conversations can show how people solve problems, handle exceptions, and make trade-offs.

Processes in motion

Tickets, project histories, CRM notes, and SOPs can connect a request to the work and outcome that followed.

A possible new use

A licensing opportunity depends on the records, their permitted use, their quality, and what a buyer needs.

Your role is the relationship.

You open the door

  • Explain the opportunity in plain language.
  • Ask a few high-level fit questions.
  • Disclose that you are a referral partner.
  • Introduce the owner to the provider team.
  • Stay helpful through the owner’s decision.

The provider leads the process

  • Checks current buyer fit and requirements.
  • Discusses scope, rights, and privacy review.
  • Explains preparation and any export process.
  • Presents proposed terms for owner review.
  • Administers licensing and partner payments.
A market example, carefully stated

Google was selected as the successful bidder in the Spirit Airlines data auction. That signals buyer interest; it does not show what another company will receive. A November 16 court filing still refers to a proposed sale. Read the court filing.

Lead with a useful conversation.

“Your business may have operational knowledge worth exploring. Let’s see whether there’s a fit before making assumptions about value.”
Keep estimates in perspective

The brochure includes dataset-value and income examples. They are illustrations, not typical earnings, offers, or forecasts. No buyer match or payment is guaranteed.
