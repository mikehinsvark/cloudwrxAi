Privacy & protection

Know the questions to ask. Keep sensitive information out of a referral conversation.

Redaction and transformation do different jobs.

REDACTION

Remove a detail

“Alice emailed Bob about Acme Corp.”

[REDACTED] emailed [REDACTED] about [REDACTED].

Useful when the detail should not appear in the shared record.

TRANSFORMATION

Substitute a detail

“Alice emailed Bob about Acme Corp.”

Andrea emailed Brian about Company A.

Consistent substitutes can preserve relationships and context.

A substitute is not a guarantee

Both examples are illustrative. Substitution can be pseudonymization rather than anonymization. Context, rare events, linked records, and retained keys may still permit identification. Ask how residual risk is assessed for the specific dataset.

The review described in the playbook

Automated preparation

Identify sensitive fields and apply agreed removal or transformation rules. Ask what the tools can and cannot detect.

Human review

Review outputs and exceptions. Ask about sampling, acceptance criteria, and how missed information is handled.

Controlled handling

Confirm recipients, permissions, transfer methods, retention, and deletion in the process and agreement.

Keep restricted material out of scope.

  • Passwords, credentials, and API keys.
  • Personal identifiers, account and payment-card details.
  • Privileged legal documents and confidential client material.
  • Medical records, protected health information, minors’ data, and biometric data.
  • Classified or export-controlled information.
  • Third-party material the company has no right to license.

Owners and advisors must determine which records can be used. A record’s presence in company systems does not by itself establish a right to license it.

Questions for the owner’s review

  1. What is included and excluded?

    Agree on scope and exclusion rules before preparation.

  2. Who approves the sample and buyer?

    Document review checkpoints and who has authority.

  3. What use does the license allow?

    Review training, evaluation, onward transfer, sublicensing, re-identification restrictions, retention, and deletion.

  4. What if a problem is found?

    Clarify how preparation pauses, issues are corrected, and incidents are handled.

The partner’s boundary

Discuss categories and questions. Let the owner, authorized IT team, legal advisors, and provider handle actual records and contractual decisions. This training does not establish legal compliance.
