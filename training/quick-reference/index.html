Quick reference

A call-side guide: what to say, how to hand off, and when to follow up.

Clear language builds trust.

Use this languageAvoid this claim
“Explore a possible fit for AI licensing.”“Your business definitely qualifies.”
“License suitable records for an agreed use.”“It’s free money from selling your data.”
“Ask how privacy and rights are reviewed.”“Everything is anonymous and risk-free.”
“The provider will discuss proposed value.”“You will get this amount.”
“Timing depends on the signed terms.”“You will be paid in 60 days.”
“Confirm fees and conditions in writing.”“There is no catch.”
“I may be compensated as a referral partner.”Leaving compensation undisclosed.
“Would a fit conversation be useful?”Pressure or manufactured urgency.

The next step, every time.

QualifyDesk staff, systems, years, decision maker.
IntroduceYour client link or a three-way call.
DiscoverThe provider reviews fit and process.
Stay closeHelp while the owner reviews and decides.

A respectful 10-day follow-up rhythm

WhenWhat to do
Day 1A personal first-touch email. Use text only with appropriate permission.
Day 2A relevant call. Leave a brief voicemail when appropriate.
Days 3–4One useful follow-up about privacy and the review process.
Days 8–10Close the loop politely. Then stop the sequence.
LaterRevisit only when appropriate to the relationship and contact preferences. An opt-out takes precedence.
