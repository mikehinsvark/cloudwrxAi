Who belongs in the conversation?

Trusted advisors

CPAs, bookkeepers, fractional CFOs, attorneys, wealth advisors, and bankers.

Deal makers

Business brokers, M&A advisors, turnaround consultants, and bankruptcy professionals.

Technology insiders

MSPs, software resellers, systems integrators, and AI automation firms.

Relationship builders

Benefits agents, business coaches, chamber leaders, and associations.

Sales organizations

B2B teams, payroll providers, merchant-services reps, and business-service firms.

Your own team

Dreams agents who can add a relevant introduction to an existing owner relationship.

Use the partner path

Share the Dreams AI Data overview, then your personal partner link. An owner seeking a fit review belongs on your client path instead.

Hi [FIRST NAME], this is [YOUR NAME]. [REFERRER] suggested we connect. I’m a referral partner with CloudWRX, which helps businesses explore AI data licensing. Since you work with owners, I thought a partner conversation might be useful. Could you send two or three times for a short three-way introduction?
Partner introduction: emailExplain the role and invite a conversation+
Make it yours before sending.
Subject: [REFERRER] suggested we connect Hi [FIRST NAME], [REFERRER] recommended that we meet. I’d like to introduce you to [CLOUDWRX CONTACT] to explore whether the CloudWRX partner program fits the work you do with business owners. The role is to make relevant introductions. The provider handles fit review and licensing, and compensation on completed deals follows your partner agreement. I’m a referral partner and may be compensated through the program. You can watch the overview at https://dreamsaidata.com/#overview. If it looks relevant, could you send two or three times for a short call? [YOUR NAME] [PHONE] Partner information: [YOUR PARTNER LINK]
Recruiting call openerFor a CPA, attorney, or IT provider+
Make it yours before sending.
[FIRST NAME], you work with many owners, so I wanted to run an idea by you. CloudWRX helps companies explore licensing suitable operational records for AI. A partner makes a relevant introduction; the provider handles the fit conversation. Any compensation depends on a completed deal and the agreement. I’m a compensated referral partner myself. Would you be open to a short conversation with their team to evaluate it? You would also want to check any professional rules that apply to referral fees.

Give every partner a sound start.

  • Review the actual agreement before describing compensation.
  • Check professional referral-fee, conflict, and disclosure rules with the appropriate advisor.
  • Do not use confidential client tax-return information to identify or approach prospects.
  • Disclose the referral relationship.
  • Keep client and partner links distinct.
  • Use income examples only as clearly labeled illustrations.
