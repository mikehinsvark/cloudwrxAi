LESSON 07 / 11
Start conversations

Texts & LinkedIn

Open a conversation. Keep the message personal, relevant, and easy to answer.

Personalize your templates +

Add your details once. They stay in this browser and fill matching brackets when you copy. Review the remaining brackets before sending.

Use the right channel

Text contacts who know you or are expecting your message, with appropriate permission. Honor opt-outs. A phone number alone is not permission for automated outreach. Review and personalize each message.

Text: someone you knowA warm first message+
Make it yours before sending.
Hi [FIRST NAME], it’s [YOUR NAME]. I found an idea that may be relevant to [COMPANY]: exploring whether suitable business records could fit an AI licensing opportunity. I’m a CloudWRX referral partner and may be paid if a deal closes. Open to a quick conversation?
Text: after an introductionUse the context from your mutual contact+
Make it yours before sending.
Hi [FIRST NAME], this is [YOUR NAME]. [MUTUAL CONTACT] suggested we connect about whether [COMPANY]’s operational records might fit an AI licensing opportunity. I’m a CloudWRX referral partner. Is this a good place to send a brief overview?
Text: after a good callSend the link they requested+
Make it yours before sending.
Great talking with you, [FIRST NAME]. Here’s my client link as promised: [YOUR CLIENT LINK] The CloudWRX team can review fit and explain the next step. Happy to help coordinate an introduction. [YOUR NAME]

Make it yours before sending.

LinkedIn: a useful postExplain the idea without a payout headline+
Make it yours before sending.
An overlooked part of business knowledge is the record of how work gets done: decisions, handoffs, tickets, and procedures. Some AI buyers license suitable operational records for training or evaluation. The questions come first: Who owns the rights? What must stay out? How is privacy reviewed? What would an agreement permit? I’m a CloudWRX referral partner and may be compensated if a deal closes. If you’re curious about exploring fit, message me for the overview. No buyer match or payment is guaranteed.
